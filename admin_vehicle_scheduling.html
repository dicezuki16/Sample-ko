1. Vehicle Calendar & Service Request

Check the date and vehicle before requesting. Coding-day requests are allowed only for departures before 10:00 AM or at/after 5:00 PM. Only final Admin Section Head approvals appear as BOOKED.

Click an available vehicle label, including a coding-day unit, to create a request. On coding days, departure must be before 10:00 AM or at/after 5:00 PM. Hover over pending/booked vehicle labels to see booking details (trip purpose is hidden). Requests pending any approval stage remain yellow and do not block the unit as a final BOOKED reservation.
